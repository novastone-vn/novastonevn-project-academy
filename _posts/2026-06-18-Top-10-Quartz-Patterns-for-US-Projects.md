Drawing from the latest industry insights and the specialized production capabilities of Nova Stone Quartz Vietnam, here are the top 10 quartz colors engineered to meet the demands of the U.S. commercial market.

## 1. [NV171: The Go-To Solid White](https://novastonevn.com/novadailyslab-nv171/)

![Nova Stone NV171](/novastonevn-project-academy/assets/images/Quartz-Slab-NV171.jpg)

NV171 stands as the industry standard for large-scale commercial and multi-family projects. Its primary strength lies in its absolute neutrality, allowing developers to coordinate it with any cabinetry or flooring palette. For high-volume purchasing, solid white quartz is the most popular choice because it simplifies procurement and ensures a clean, modern aesthetic that appeals to a broad range of tenants and hotel guests.

## 2. [NV393: The Granular Masterpiece](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv393-link)

![Nova Stone NV393](/assets/images/NV393.jpg)

Marketed as "Redefining Scale and Value," NV393 is a strategic choice for value engineering in U.S. projects. Granular slabs are historically more consistent in color and pattern than veined slabs, making them ideal for massive developments where hundreds of identical units must be installed. This pattern provides the premium feel of stone at a price point that maximizes ROI for developers.

## 3. [NV905: Modern Multi-Unit Calacatta](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv905-link)

![Nova Stone NV905](/assets/images/NV905.jpg)

While bold marble looks are popular in retail, the NV905 offers a softer interpretation of Calacatta specifically designed for modern multi-unit projects. Its subtle, flowing veins are easier to book-match and fabricate for large kitchen islands, ensuring that the design remains sophisticated without being visually overwhelming in smaller urban apartment layouts.

## 4. [NV865: Large-Scale Scaling](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv865-link)

![Nova Stone NV865](/assets/images/NV865.jpg)

NV865 is a unique pattern designed to "get better as the project gets bigger". This makes it exceptionally suited for expansive commercial expanses, such as long hotel corridors, airport lounges, or grand lobbies. The pattern is engineered to avoid "stamping" or repetitive visual breaks, maintaining a seamless, natural flow over dozens of linear feet.

## 5. [NV770 (Muted): High-Volume Subtlety](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv770-link)

![Nova Stone NV770](/assets/images/NV770.jpg)

Belonging to the Fine Linear Series, the muted NV770 focuses on "The Power of Subtlety". In professional environments like corporate offices, medical clinics, or educational facilities, a muted pattern is often preferred over bold designs. It offers the durability of quartz while providing a calming, professional backdrop that supports long-term commercial demand.

## 6. [NV391 (Polished) / NV404 (Leathered): Pure Black](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv391-link)

![Nova Stone NV391 and NV404](/assets/images/NV391.jpg)

Pure black quartz, such as NV391 and NV404, provides the aesthetic discipline required for high-end accent surfaces. Whether used for executive bar tops or boutique hotel vanities, the availability of both Polished and Leathered finishes allows designers to specify texture based on the specific lighting and tactile requirements of a commercial space.

## 7. [NV883: Balanced Calacatta Gold](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv883-link)

![Nova Stone NV883](/assets/images/NV883.jpg)

For hospitality projects seeking a luxury feel, NV883 offers a "Balanced" Calacatta Gold application. Gold-veined quartz is a top-tier trend in U.S. luxury condos, but the key to its long-term demand is balance. NV883 provides enough warmth to feel high-end without being so trendy that it risks looking dated in five years.

## 8. [NV741: Classic Carrara Whisper](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv741-link)

![Nova Stone NV741](/assets/images/NV741.jpg)

Described as a "Whisper of Classic Carrara," NV741 is a staple for hospitality bathroom vanities. Its delicate veining mimics the most famous Italian marbles, providing a familiar "residential" comfort to hotel guests. Because the pattern is so light, it is highly versatile and hides water spots or minor wear-and-tear better than solid or heavily veined surfaces.

## 9. [NV678: Invisible Elegance](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv678-link)

![Nova Stone NV678](/assets/images/NV678.jpg)

Part of the Fine Linear Series, NV678 represents "The Art of Invisible Elegance". This pattern is highly suitable for minimalist retail environments and "Class A" office spaces where the design goal is a high-end feel that does not distract from the brand’s products or architecture. Its minimalist nature ensures it will remain in demand through multiple design cycles.

## 10. [NV381: Starlit Galaxy](https://www.novastonevn.com/nv381-link)

![Nova Stone NV381](/assets/images/NV381.jpg)

NV381 is a granular slab with subtle reflective properties, described as a "Starlit Galaxy". This is a prime choice for hospitality and entertainment venues—such as bars, theaters, or boutique lobbies—where dramatic lighting is often used. The subtle shimmer enhances the material's depth, creating an engaging tactile experience for patrons.

The most specified quartz colors are not always the most visually dramatic. In many commercial environments, the winning choice is often the color that delivers the best balance of aesthetics, durability, maintenance efficiency, and long-term availability.
